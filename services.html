Challenge
Secure funding for an acquisition with a pack lenders could assess quickly.

Commercial terms

Retail lease and heads of terms

Challenge
Agree workable lease and deal terms on a retail site.
Approach
Compared terms, prepared a negotiation brief and led the commercial discussions, with the solicitor handling legal drafting.
